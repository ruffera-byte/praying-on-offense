Move or back up your prayer map

Your map and notes are saved in the browser you’re using. Everyone who opens the site starts with their own map. Sharing the website link shares the tool. Your entries stay in your browser.

  1. Open your saved prayer map and tap Export backup. Your backup is a file named prayer-map-YYYY-MM-DD.json.
  2. Keep the file where your new browser can find it. If you’re switching browsers on the same phone, use the same downloaded file. If you’re switching devices, move it with AirDrop, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or another file-sharing method.
  3. Open Praying on Offense in the browser you want to use. Tap Export / Import → Import backup, choose the .json file, and confirm. The import replaces the map in that browser. Export its existing map first if you want to keep a copy.

Where to find the backup on your phone

  • Safari on iPhone or iPad: In the file picker, tap Browse and look in iCloud Drive → Downloads or On My iPhone → Downloads, depending on your Safari download settings.
  • Chrome on iPhone or iPad: If Chrome offers a choice when downloading, save to Files and remember the folder. In the import picker, tap Browse and look in that folder. If Chrome shows a preview, use Share → Save to Files if offered.
  • Chrome on Android: In the file picker, look in Recent or Downloads. You can also find the backup in Chrome’s ⋮ → Downloads menu.
  • Other browsers or a computer: Check the browser’s Downloads menu to find where it saved the file. In the import picker, browse to that folder or to the cloud folder where you moved it.

If you don’t see the backup, search your device’s Files app for prayer-map. Keep the backup somewhere private because it contains your notes. Export a backup before clearing site data. Private browsing may erase entries when you close it.
