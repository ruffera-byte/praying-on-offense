Move or back up your prayer map

Your map and notes are saved in the browser you’re using. Everyone who opens the site starts with their own map. Sharing the website link shares the tool. Your entries stay in your browser.

  1. On your current browser, choose Export backup to download your prayer map.
  2. Move the file to your other device if needed, then open this same website in the browser you want to use.
  3. Choose Import backup and select the file. Importing replaces the entries in that browser.

Export a backup before clearing site data, which can erase your map. Private browsing may erase entries when you close it. Keep your backup somewhere private because it contains your notes.
